    How a report is built

    Four stages. Nothing hidden.

    Every step is auditable. If the logic doesn't hold, you'll see it.

    01 Classify

    Establish what it is

    Product type, who signs the purchase order, whether it goes through FDA at all. Get this wrong and everything else is wrong too.

    02 Retrieve

    Find the denominator

    Prevalence registries, facility counts, grant awards, patents, literature. What's worth pulling depends on the buyer. Every number keeps a reference.

    03 Derive

    Build the cells

    Pick dimensions that change price or adoption and build a cell for each. The math runs in code, not in a paragraph where it can't be checked.

    04 Contest

    Check it, then correct it

    If numbers don't hold, the verifier says so. Change any assumption you know better — the rest of the model adjusts immediately.
